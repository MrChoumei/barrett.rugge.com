Welcome to my website!

I'm Barrett, a first-year undergraduate at the University of Washington. My major is Japanese, and my interests are as follows:

- Japanese (現代語, 和文, and 漢文)
- Philosophy (most everything, excluding ethics)
- Chinese (though I'm still a beginner)
- Literature
- Poetry
- And a lot more, which I'm afraid the margins are too narrow to contain.

This website is primarily a means of publication: I will post projects I'm working on so anyone, if they want to use my work, can.

Secondarily, it is a repository of sorts. In my life I have witnessed people stumbling over subjects they could sprint through, only for a lack of resources. To help a little, if I can, I will post those that I've compiled.

Head to the [Articles](/posts/) page to find my projects.
